- v2 media data lives in child tables (song_lyrics, song_audio_versions, song_videos) keyed by song + language; songs columns stay as legacy defaults — why: non-destructive multi-language extension.
- Motion artwork lives in song_motion_artwork rather than songs or song_videos — why: keep short muted artwork separate from full-length Video mode media.
- Game hub card presentation uses app_settings and a shared card component for listener display and CMS previews — why: admin edits remain consistent without changing game rules or routes.
- Tilted photos load eagerly with async decoding and bundled per-game fallbacks — why: reliable artwork across views without failed-request loops.
- Games hover and press styling is scoped under games-interactive in global CSS, with token-based effects and reduced-motion overrides — why: keep CMS previews and animations intact.
- Challenge leaderboard and progress presentation is scoped under challenge-arcade using shared game tone/motion tokens and reduced-motion overrides — why: restyle rankings without touching challenge rules.
- Challenge banner and detail heading share game_hub_cards artwork/colour settings and the tilted-photo control; the detail keeps live challenge copy/countdown, challengeStats uses a shared listener/CMS preview, and operational values remain in challenges — why: consistent CMS presentation must not alter competition rules or prizes.
- Plays are logged only via the record_play RPC into play_events (no client inserts); discovery reads go through get_trending/get_quick_picks/get_daily_discover — why: privacy and consistent counters.
- Playback continuity is stored locally and restored paused without logging a new play — why: preserve position across navigation and app exits without inflating analytics.
- Stem separation runs through a queue in song_audio_versions (kind='instrumental' is the job row), processed by the stem-worker function on a cron that arms on enqueue and unschedules when drained — why: bounded, single-flight, retry-safe background AI work with a persisted pause on billing errors.
- Song, Karaoke, mini-player, and Video playback timelines use the shared PlaybackProgress component — why: progress tracks and position markers must remain visually consistent.
- Admin-managed look & home layout (theme colours, home section order/visibility, curated rails) live in app_settings via src/lib/siteSettings.ts; playlist card colours live in playlists.card_color — why: no hardcoded styling, CMS controls it.
- Stage Mode presentation media, selectable background library, fallback colour, and song-level overrides live in app_settings via src/lib/siteSettings.ts — why: admins manage reusable stage choices and distinct song backgrounds without hardcoding them in the screen.
- Renewal reminders are milestone-deduped in renewal_reminders and sent by the process-expired-subscriptions function (in-app notifications + OneSignal push); the home RenewalBanner only reads subscription state — why: one reminder per milestone, no client-side dupes.
- Community data lives in communities/community_members/community_posts/community_post_likes/community_post_comments; reads, likes and comments are members-only via is_community_member/can_view_community_post (admin override), member counts via get_community_member_counts, and inserts are throttled per user by the enforce_community_rate_limit trigger — why: private, spam-resistant communities without a platform rate limiter.
- Community card background colours are stored by community ID in app_settings and applied as scoped CSS tokens — why: admins can edit presentation without changing membership or content authorization.
- Community directory and detail views share the Community screen with a route parameter; profile images use communities.cover_url and the existing storage picker — why: preserve a single membership/feed implementation and reuse admin upload authorization.
- Community-scoped moderation grants live in community_moderators, assigned only through admin-validated RPCs and checked by RLS; membership roles are not moderation authority — why: prevent self-promotion and cross-community access.
- Playback resolves the listener's preferred lyrics language (user_preferences.preferred_languages[0]) in PlayerContext before play: ready song_audio_versions (kind='full') in that language swap the audio URL and its song_lyrics swap the lyrics — why: listeners hear and read their chosen language without per-song action.
- Optional listener features use the shared FeatureProvider and featureFlags registry backed by individual app_settings keys; reuse existing keys, gate routes/navigation/player actions, refresh after admin writes and every 30 seconds, and retain admin CMS access — why: consistent switches without duplicate settings or content deletion; feature switches are presentation controls, not substitutes for server authorization.
- The referral switch is enforced server-side by referrals_enabled() in referral RPCs/trigger — why: not client-bypassable.
