- v2 media data lives in child tables (song_lyrics, song_audio_versions, song_videos) keyed by song + language; songs columns stay as legacy defaults — why: non-destructive multi-language extension.
- Motion artwork lives in song_motion_artwork rather than songs or song_videos — why: keep short muted artwork separate from full-length Video mode media.
- Games/challenge presentation (hub cards, challenge banner/heading, challengeStats) uses app_settings with shared listener/CMS preview components; tilted photos load eagerly with bundled fallbacks; effects are scoped under games-interactive / challenge-arcade CSS with reduced-motion overrides; operational values stay in challenges — why: CMS styling never alters game rules or prizes.
- Plays are logged only via the record_play RPC into play_events (no client inserts); discovery reads go through get_trending/get_quick_picks/get_daily_discover — why: privacy and consistent counters.
- Playback continuity is stored locally and restored paused without logging a new play — why: preserve position across navigation and app exits without inflating analytics.
- Stem separation runs through a queue in song_audio_versions (kind='instrumental' is the job row), processed by the stem-worker function on a cron that arms on enqueue and unschedules when drained — why: bounded, single-flight, retry-safe background AI work with a persisted pause on billing errors.
- Song, Karaoke, mini-player, and Video playback timelines use the shared PlaybackProgress component — why: progress tracks and position markers must remain visually consistent.
- Admin-managed look & home layout (theme colours, home section order/visibility, curated rails) live in app_settings via src/lib/siteSettings.ts; playlist card colours live in playlists.card_color — why: no hardcoded styling, CMS controls it.
- Stage Mode presentation media, selectable background library, fallback colour, and song-level overrides live in app_settings via src/lib/siteSettings.ts — why: admins manage reusable stage choices and distinct song backgrounds without hardcoding them in the screen.
- Renewal reminders are milestone-deduped in renewal_reminders and sent by the process-expired-subscriptions function (in-app notifications + OneSignal push); the home RenewalBanner only reads subscription state — why: one reminder per milestone, no client-side dupes.
- Community data (communities, members, posts, likes, comments) is members-only via is_community_member/can_view_community_post (admin override), counts via get_community_member_counts, inserts throttled by enforce_community_rate_limit; moderators live in community_moderators, assigned only by admin-validated RPCs (membership roles grant no moderation); card colours live in app_settings by community ID; directory/detail share the Community screen and communities.cover_url — why: private, spam-resistant communities with presentation separate from authorization.
- Playback resolves the listener's preferred lyrics language (user_preferences.preferred_languages[0]) in PlayerContext before play: ready song_audio_versions (kind='full') in that language swap the audio URL and its song_lyrics swap the lyrics — why: listeners hear and read their chosen language without per-song action.
- Optional listener features use the shared FeatureProvider and featureFlags registry backed by individual app_settings keys; reuse existing keys, gate routes/navigation/player actions, refresh after admin writes, when the app returns to screen (throttled) and every few minutes while visible, and retain admin CMS access — why: consistent switches without duplicate settings or content deletion, without constant background polling; feature switches are presentation controls, not substitutes for server authorization.
- Lists that can exceed 1,000 rows (songs, pickers, admin tables) load through fetchAllRows in src/lib/fetchAll.ts with a stable `.order("id")` tiebreaker; no new background polling under 1 minute, RLS policies wrap auth.uid()/has_role in `(SELECT ...)` — why: the backend caps each request at 1,000 rows, and per-row checks and tight polling are the main load at scale.
- The referral switch is enforced server-side by referrals_enabled() in referral RPCs/trigger — why: not client-bypassable.
- Online content search goes through the search_content RPC (weighted simple+unaccent tsvector, trigram titles) via src/lib/contentSearch.ts with a shared 250 ms debounce; client-side fuzzy filtering is only for the offline downloads library — why: one consistent multi-language ranking everywhere.
- The mini player's tucked-away state lives in the localStorage-backed store src/lib/playerVisibility.ts (usePlayerHidden), read by both PlayerBar and AppLayout — why: the screen's bottom spacing must shrink with the player and the choice must survive navigation and reloads without a new context.
